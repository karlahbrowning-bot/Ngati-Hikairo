# Ngāti Hikairo Ahu Whenua Trust
## AI Operating Manual

This repository holds the AI Operating Manual for **Ngāti Hikairo Ahu Whenua Trust** — an Ahu Whenua Trust constituted under Te Ture Whenua Māori Act 1993, registered with the Māori Land Court.

---

## What is an AI Operating Manual?

An AI Operating Manual is a set of plain-English documents that describe how the Trust operates — who we are, how we communicate, how we do things, and what our key terms mean.

Loading these files into an AI tool (such as Claude) means the AI understands the Trust's context, tone, and processes — and can help draft communications, meeting minutes, grant letters, and other documents in the Trust's voice.

## The four sections of the Operating Manual

### COMPANY.MD — Ko Wai Mātou
Who the Trust is, the land blocks we hold, our vision, purpose and values, who our beneficiaries are, how we are governed, and our trustee structure.

### BRAND.MD — How We Communicate
Our visual identity (black and white, clean and professional), our voice (open, honest, transparent), our core writing principles, banned phrases, and a before/after example of the Trust's tone.

### PROCESSES.MD — How We Do Things
Step-by-step processes for:
- Running an Annual General Meeting (AGM)
- Processing grant applications (Higher Education, Sports, Kaumatua, Tangihanga)
- Running quarterly Trustee meetings

### GLOSSARY.MD — Kuputaka
Definitions for te reo Māori terms, legal and governance terms, land block names, grant types, and the Trust's tools and schedules.
